**> What are my two options for the EC$500 that gets invested — leaving it in shares, or moving it to the savings side?**

The EC$500 is not described as giving you two personal choices. It is the investment component of the ASPIRE contribution, while a separate EC$500 goes into savings.

Here is how it works. The investment EC$500 purchases shares in local, government-owned entities and public enterprises. This gives the beneficiary ownership as a shareholder. If the entities grow and pay dividends, the shareholder may benefit. The investment pool is managed centrally, rather than allowing beneficiaries to select individual shares.

The published information does not state that a beneficiary may move the investment EC$500 into the savings account, or switch between the shares and savings components. That transfer option is beyond what is published, so the ASPIRE team is the right source: aspire@gov.kn, +1 (869) 667-5566 or +1 (869) 762-1947. Quote ASP-50653 so they have the context.
