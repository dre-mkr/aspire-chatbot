**> What is a share?**

Think of a company as a big bakery cut into many slices. A share is one small piece of ownership in that company.

When someone buys a share, they own a slice of the business. If the company grows and becomes worth more, that share may become worth more too. The shareholder may also receive part of the company’s profits. These payments are called dividends.

For example, if a bakery had 1,000 equal slices and someone owned one slice, they would own one thousandth of the bakery. In ASPIRE, EC$500 was invested in shares for students. This gives them real ownership in local companies.

Shares matter because they show how investing can make someone an owner, not only a saver. What might happen to a share if the company grows and earns profits?
